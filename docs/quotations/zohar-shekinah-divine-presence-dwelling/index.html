Latin Quotation

Shekinah est praesentia Dei, quae inter duos mundos manet, et ab saeculo in saeculum descendit super mundos inferiores.
Translation: "The Shekinah is the presence of God, which dwells between two worlds, and from age to age descends upon the lower worlds."

Source Information

Original Text: Unknown Source

Cited by Scholar: François Secret

Work: Zohar or Kabbalistic interpretation

Scholarly Commentary

⌘ Historical Context
Secret shows how Renaissance Christian Kabbalists integrated Zoharic mysticism with Christian theology, interpreting Shekinah as feminine divine aspect compatible with Mary-theology. This allowed educated practitioners to pursue Kabbalistic magic within Christian framework, profoundly influencing Renaissance and early modern magical philosophy.
— François Secret
⌘ Linguistic Analysis
Latin adaptation of Hebrew Kabbalistic terminology. 'Shekinah' remains untranslated, maintaining mystical resonance. 'Praesentia Dei' (presence of God) translates Hebrew shekhinat yah. The phrase 'inter duos mundos' (between two worlds) reflects Kabbalistic concept of Shekinah dwelling at interface of finite and infinite. Present tense suggests timeless principle.
— François Secret
⌘ Magical Significance
If Shekinah descends regularly into lower worlds, then through proper contemplation and theurgic operation, magus-mystic can invoke her presence. Shekinah becomes intermediary through which human will touches divine—not through compulsion but through mystical receptivity.
— François Secret
