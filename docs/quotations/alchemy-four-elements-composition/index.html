Latin Quotation

Ignis, Aer, Aqua, Terra: haec quattuor elementa omnium rerum compositionem constituunt, et in eorum mutatione omnis generatio et corruptio versatur.
Translation: "Fire, Air, Water, Earth: these four elements constitute the composition of all things, and in their change all generation and corruption revolve."
