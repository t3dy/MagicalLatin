Latin Quotation

Quod est superius, est sicut quod inferius; et quod est inferius, est sicut quod est superius, ad perpetranda miracula rei unius.
Translation: "That which is above is like that which is below; and that which is below is like that which is above, to accomplish the miracles of the one thing."

Source Information

Original Text: Grimoires - A History of Magic Books (2009) - libgen.li

Cited by Scholar: Owen Davies

Work: Emerald Tablet (Tabula Smaragdina)

Scholarly Commentary

⌘ Linguistic Analysis
Latin translation of Arabic original (Balinas/Hermes Trismegistus). The bidirectional structure (superius/inferius) reflects the principle of correspondence.
— Owen Davies
⌘ Magical Significance
Most fundamental principle in Western magic. Establishes the theoretical basis for sympathetic magic and correspondential operations. Justifies the construction of talismans, sigils, and ritual correspondences.
— Owen Davies
⌘ Scholarly Interpretation
Davies shows how this principle enabled European mages to construct complex systems of correspondence linking celestial bodies, metals, stones, plants, and spiritual entities. This principle remains central to modern magical practice.
— Owen Davies
