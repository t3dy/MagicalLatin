Latin Quotation

Ignis, Aer, Aqua, Terra sunt quattuor elementa ex quibus omnia corpora composita sunt, et in quae omnia denuo resolvuntur.
Translation: "Fire, Air, Water, and Earth are the four elements from which all bodies are composed, and into which all things are again resolved."

Source Information

Original Text: False Lying Spirits and Angels of Light Egil Asprem on Dr Rudd

Cited by Scholar: Egil Asprem

Work: Alchemical theory

Scholarly Commentary

⌘ Linguistic Analysis
Classical Latin formulation of Aristotelian physics, central to medieval and Renaissance alchemy. The symmetry of composition/dissolution reflects cyclical understanding of matter and spirit.
— Egil Asprem
⌘ Magical Significance
The four elements provided the theoretical framework for understanding transmutation (both physical and spiritual). Magical operations aligned with elemental correspondences to activate specific qualities and transformations.
— Egil Asprem
⌘ Scholarly Interpretation
Asprem connects elemental theory to both magical practice and Christian mysticism, showing how alchemical language provided a vocabulary for discussing spiritual transformation and divine action.
— Egil Asprem
