## Coverage Notes

**Ancient sources well represented**: Greek Magical Papyri, Latin defixiones, Orphic gold tablets, Mesopotamian exorcism, Aramaic bowl spells.

**Medieval sources well represented**: Picatrix, Ars Notoria, Liber Iuratus, Munich Manual, Clavicula Salomonis, Lemegeton.

- Babylonian astrology Latin reception (Rochberg material)
